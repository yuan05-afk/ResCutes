# Spoken script - ResCutes 2-minute demo

Read only the **SAY** lines. Stage directions in brackets are for you, not the microphone.

Pace: about 130 words per minute. Total: about 260 words. Finish talking by 1:58. Hold the last frame for the end card.

If you run long, skip the Aurora underpass sentence. If you run short, pause on **Top shelter match** before clicking Adoption.

---

## 0:00 to 0:18 - Landing

[Tab 1. Hold the hero. Slow-scroll the four workflow cards. Do not click Sign In.]

**SAY:**

Today, a hurt animal on EDSA is a Facebook post, three group chats, and a shelter that may already be full.

ResCutes connects citizens, rescuers, shelters, and vets on one case, from a street report to a home.

Phone for the field. Dashboard for dispatch. Same animal. No copy-paste.

---

## 0:18 to 0:42 - Citizen phone (Aha 1)

[Switch to Tab 3. Show the home map. Tap Report. Walk Photo, Location, Animal, Condition. Tap Dog, Severe, Traffic. Stop on Review. Do not submit.]

**SAY:**

This is the citizen phone. The person who sees the animal is not a dispatcher.

Photo, pin, then three taps: species, injury, danger.

Urgency and shelter matching come from these fields, not from a wall of comments.

---

## 0:42 to 1:05 - Staff dashboard (Aha 2)

[Switch to Tab 2. Pause on Operations Overview. Pan the map. Open the EDSA Cubao case.]

**SAY:**

This is 7 a.m. for a shelter coordinator. Capacity. Active cases. What is still unassigned.

The map is live. Pins are real cases. Staff work a queue, not Messenger.

---

## 1:05 to 1:35 - EDSA case already with a rescuer (Aha 3)

[Stay on the staff case. Point in order: location, Urgency, With rescuer, Rescuer James Chen accepted, Destination PAWS PARC, Top shelter match. Optional: the Aurora underpass note. Do not look for a destination dropdown.]

**SAY:**

This aspin was hit on the EDSA Cubao service road. Severe injury. Highway traffic. The urgency is explained, not guessed.

James Chen already accepted. The case is with a rescuer.

Destination is PAWS PARC. Not the nearest pin. The engine ranked medical capability, remaining beds, species, distance, and workload. PAWS can treat trauma, and it still has space.

Top shelter match is the receipt. You can see why this facility won.

The field note is on the same record. Approach from the Aurora underpass. No second chat.

---

## 1:35 to 1:50 - Luna

[Staff sidebar: Adoption. Find Luna. Do not start an application.]

**SAY:**

We did not stop at report submitted. Luna is the other end of the same record. Examined, medically cleared, ready for a family.

---

## 1:50 to 2:00 - Close

[Cut back to the landing hero, or hold a split of phone and dashboard. Stop talking at 1:58.]

**SAY:**

One photo. One scored case. One ranked shelter. One named animal ready for a home.

That is ResCutes: from report to rescue, care, and adoption.

The live demo is up. Open rescutes.vercel.app, pick a demo role, and try it.
